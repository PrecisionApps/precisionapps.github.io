Support

Answers to the common questions, how to manage your subscription, and how to reach us.

Starting a book

Tap the daily prompt on Home, pick a prompt or one of your saved ideas in Ideas, or create a blank book in Library. The idea you started from is kept as the book's premise and shown above the editor, and the AI tools use it so suggestions fit your story. Add, rename, reorder or delete chapters from the chapter list in the editor.

Using the AI tools

Open a chapter and use the bar above the keyboard: Ask AI, Rewrite and Grammar, or the wand when the keyboard is hidden. Each result opens as a preview. Tap a paragraph to leave it out, tap Edit to change it, then Apply, or Discard to keep your chapter as it was. Right after applying, you can undo the change. If a request fails, check your internet connection and tap Try Again; your prompt is kept.

Dictation isn't working

Storivo needs microphone access. Open the iOS Settings app → Storivo → Microphone and turn it on. Dictation needs an internet connection and records up to five minutes at a time; stop and start again for longer passages.

Daily reminders aren't arriving

Turn on Daily Reminders and choose a time in Storivo → Profile → the gear button (Settings). Then check the iOS Settings app → Notifications → Storivo. Focus modes can hold notifications back; allow Storivo in your Focus if you want reminders during it.

Backing up and exporting

Your writing is stored on your device and included in your iCloud or computer backup. To keep a copy anywhere else, use the Export button in the editor for a chapter or book, or Profile → Settings → Export All Writing, and save it with the share sheet. Deleting Storivo deletes its library, so export first if you plan to remove the app.

Restoring Storivo Pro

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open any Pro feature (an AI tool or the microphone) to show the Storivo Pro screen, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Canceling your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Subscriptions can't be canceled from inside the app, and deleting the app does not cancel one.

  • iPhone or iPad: open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Storivo → Cancel Subscription.
  • App Store app: tap your profile picture → Subscriptions → Storivo → Cancel Subscription.
  • Mac: open the App Store → click your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions → Manage → Storivo.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. If you are on a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid being charged. Apple's guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
