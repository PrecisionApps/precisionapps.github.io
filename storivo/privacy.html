Storivo

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 28, 2026

Storivo is a writing app for iPhone and iPad, published by Kaan Yıldız (PrecisionApps). Your books stay on your device. This policy explains what leaves it, when, and why.

1. No account

Storivo has no sign-up and no login. We never ask for your email address, phone number, contacts or location. The optional display name you can type in Settings stays on your device.

2. Your writing stays on your device

Your books, chapters, premises, saved ideas, favorite prompts, writing streak and settings are stored only in Storivo's storage on your device. Storivo does not upload your library to any server and does not sync it. As with any app, this data is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups under your Apple Account. When you export a chapter or a book, the file goes only where you send it from the share sheet.

3. AI writing tools

When you use an AI feature (Ask AI, Rewrite, Grammar, writing an opening, Inspire Me, or generating prompts and stories in Ideas), Storivo sends the request over an encrypted connection to our API gateway, operated on Zuplo, which forwards it to OpenAI to generate the reply. A request contains your instruction, the passage or chapter text the tool works on, and the book's title and premise so the reply fits your story. It does not contain your name, email or any account identifier.

We do not store the content of these requests on our own systems, and we do not use your writing to train models. OpenAI processes API requests under its Privacy Policy and API data commitments, which state that API data is not used to train its models by default and may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. AI features only run when you start them.

4. Dictation and the microphone

Storivo asks for microphone access the first time you tap the microphone, and records only while dictation is active (up to five minutes at a time). The recording is saved to a temporary file on your device, sent through the same gateway to OpenAI's speech-to-text model with your language setting, and the transcribed text is placed into your chapter. The temporary audio file is deleted from your device once dictation finishes. You can withdraw microphone access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

5. Analytics

To understand how Storivo is used and to improve it, the app sends anonymous usage events to a Google Firebase database that we operate. These events include:

  • app opens, sessions and screens viewed;
  • features used, such as creating a book or running an AI tool, whether an AI request succeeded, and coarse size ranges such as "100–499 words";
  • the options you choose in onboarding questions;
  • paywall views and dismissals, and purchase events (product, price, currency, transaction identifier and offer type);
  • device model, iOS version, app version, language, region setting and App Store country;
  • a random install identifier created by the app, and the anonymous RevenueCat customer identifier.

Analytics never include the text you write: no titles, premises, prompts, chapter content or dictated words. They are not linked to your identity, are not used for advertising, and are not combined with data from other companies' apps. Storivo does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.

6. Subscriptions and purchases

Storivo Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. Storivo uses RevenueCat to confirm purchases and keep your Pro access in sync. RevenueCat receives the App Store transaction information, an anonymous customer identifier, the install identifier described above, and basic device and app information, under the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Apple may share aggregated sales reports with developers.

7. Notifications and ratings

Daily writing reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device at the time you choose; they are not sent through any server. You can turn them off in Storivo's Settings or in the iOS Settings app. Requests to rate Storivo use Apple's built-in rating sheet; Apple handles any rating you leave.

8. Service providers

  • OpenAI: generates AI replies and transcribes dictation.
  • Zuplo: runs the API gateway that forwards AI and dictation requests.
  • Google Firebase: stores anonymous analytics events.
  • RevenueCat: verifies purchases and subscription status.
  • Apple: App Store payments, subscriptions, rating sheet and notifications.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete any book, chapter or idea inside Storivo. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. Analytics events are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the app. Because they are anonymous, we cannot look them up by name; if you want us to delete them, write to us and we will remove what we can associate with your install.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and California law, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to its processing. To make a request, email us at the address below. We will answer within 30 days.

11. Children's privacy

Storivo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Storivo, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
