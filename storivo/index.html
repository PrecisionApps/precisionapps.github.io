Storivo

Write the book you keep thinking about.

Storivo is a writing app for iPhone and iPad. Start a book from an idea, write it chapter by chapter, and call on an AI writing partner when you're stuck.

Download on the App Store

Books and chapters

Every book keeps its premise, its chapters and their order. Add, rename, reorder and delete chapters, switch between them from the editor, and read the whole book back in a clean reader view.

AI tools that ask before they change anything

Ask the AI for help with a scene, write an opening, rewrite a passage or fix grammar and punctuation. Every suggestion opens as a preview: keep the paragraphs you like, edit them, or discard the lot. Your chapter only changes when you tap Apply.

Speak instead of type

Tap the microphone and dictate. Storivo transcribes what you say and drops it into your chapter.

Ideas when the page is blank

A daily prompt on Home, a library of writing prompts by genre, Inspire Me for fresh ones, and your own saved ideas with tags and search. When an idea is ready, turn it into a new book.

Keep the habit

Daily reminders at the time you choose, a writing streak, and insights on how much you've written. Export a chapter or a whole book as text or a rich-text file whenever you like.
