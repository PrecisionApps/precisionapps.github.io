Already

The truest way to remember a day is backwards.

Already is a journal you write in reverse. Each night, a gentle guide asks one question — “What’s the last thing you did?” — and walks you back through your evening, one step at a time, all the way to the morning. The moments you’d normally skip past come back on the walk.

What Already keeps

  • The day, unwound — your night’s walk woven into a short story you can reread forever.
  • Threads — what your weeks keep repeating: the kettle, the walks, the people your evenings reach for.
  • An archive — every kept day, searchable.
  • A quiet knock — an evening reminder, if you want one.

No account. No ads. Your days stay on your device.
