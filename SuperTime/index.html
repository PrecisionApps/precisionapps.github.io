Support

Every task is a deadline. Here's how to get the most out of SuperTime, and how to reach us.

Typing deadlines

Write one line the way you'd say it: “pay rent friday”, “call the bank tomorrow morning”, “submit the report by 5pm”. The strip above the field shows the deadline SuperTime understood before you add it; tap it to adjust. Words like “tonight”, “morning” and “end of day” follow the hours set in Settings → Your day. “Next friday” means the Friday of next week; “this friday” or just “friday” means the coming one.

Repeating deadlines

Add a repeat in your words — “every month on the 1st”, “every weekday at 9”, “gym every monday 7am”, “daily”, “every other tuesday” — or open a task and choose Repeat. When you finish a repeating task, the next one takes its place. Snoozing moves only the current one; the schedule stays the same.

Widgets and Control Center

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and search for SuperTime. The Next Up widget comes in small, medium and large sizes for the Home Screen and in three shapes for the Lock Screen. Tap a bead on a widget to finish that task. To add the New Deadline control, open Control Center, tap + and search for SuperTime.

Reminders aren't arriving

Check Settings → Notifications → SuperTime in iOS, and Settings → Reminders inside SuperTime. Focus modes can hold notifications back; allow SuperTime in your Focus if you want deadline alerts during it.

The assistant

Brain dumps are always free. Planning, reflowing, changes and questions use assists: five are free, and SuperTime Unlimited makes them unlimited. With Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later on supported devices) the assistant uses Apple's on-device model; otherwise it uses SuperTime's on-device engine. Nothing leaves your device either way.

Subscriptions

To restore a purchase on a new device, open SuperTime → Settings → Restore purchases. To cancel or change your plan, open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → SuperTime, or SuperTime → Settings → Manage subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
