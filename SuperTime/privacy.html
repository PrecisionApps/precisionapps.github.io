SuperTime

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

SuperTime is a deadline app that keeps everything on your iPhone or iPad. This policy explains what that means in practice.

1. Information we do not collect

SuperTime has no account, no server and no sign-in. We do not collect, store or receive your tasks, deadlines, settings, contacts, location or usage data. The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and it does not share data with data brokers or third parties.

2. Information stored on your device

Your tasks, deadlines, repeat settings, finished tasks and preferences (such as the hours of your day and reminder settings) are stored only in SuperTime's storage on your device. To show your next deadlines in widgets, the app also writes a small summary of your upcoming tasks to a shared container on the same device that only SuperTime and its widgets can read. This data is included in your device's own backups (iCloud or computer backups) under your Apple Account, as with any app; SuperTime does not sync it anywhere.

3. The assistant

When you plan your day, reflow slipped tasks, make changes by saying them or ask a question, your request is understood on your device: by Apple's on-device foundation model where Apple Intelligence is available (iOS 26 or later on supported devices), or by SuperTime's own on-device rules engine otherwise. Your words are never sent to us or to any third party.

4. Siri and Shortcuts

If you add deadlines with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes your voice request under Apple's Privacy Policy and passes the resulting text to SuperTime on your device.

5. Calendar

Calendar access is off until you turn it on in SuperTime's Settings. If you allow it, SuperTime reads today's events from the calendars on your device to show them on your day and to plan around them. Events are read on your device only; they are not copied into SuperTime's storage and are never sent anywhere. You can withdraw access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

6. Subscriptions and purchase data

SuperTime Unlimited is sold through the App Store. Apple processes payments; we never see your payment details. The app checks with the App Store, on your device, whether your subscription is active. Apple may share aggregated, non-identifying sales reports with developers.

7. Notifications

Deadline reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. They are not sent through any server. You can turn them off in SuperTime's Settings or in the iOS Settings app at any time.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete any task in the app, and clear finished tasks in Settings. Finished tasks are removed automatically after 30 days. Deleting SuperTime from your device removes all of its data, including the widget summary.

9. Children's privacy

SuperTime does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
