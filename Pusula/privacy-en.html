Pusula AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

In short: Pusula AI does not ask for an account, does not show ads, does not track you and does not sell your personal data. Your tasks, learning paths, practice and templates are stored on your device. The text you write is sent to our server only to produce a response for you.

What stays on your device

  • Your tasks and answers, your learning paths, your practice and your prompt templates are kept in the app’s local storage on your device.
  • Your profile settings (the name you optionally enter and your experience level) are stored only on your device; no name is sent to the server.

“Reset data” in the Progress tab deletes all of these. Deleting the app removes everything the app has stored. Because there is no account, no profile or history belonging to you is kept on the server.

What is sent to the server

The tool recommendation, learning path, prompt evaluation, prompt improvement, “Try it together” and tool comparison features run on the Pusula server. When you use these features, the task text you write, your answers to the questions, your experience level and the prompt you ask to have evaluated are transmitted to our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

  • This text is processed only to produce a response to your request; it is not stored on the Pusula server and is not linked to any identity.
  • To produce the response, the text may be forwarded by our server to an AI model provider. The provider does not use this content to train its models; it may retain it for a limited period under its own policies in order to prevent abuse.
  • The server and hosting infrastructure keep standard logs such as IP address, request time and technical error records for a limited period for security, rate limiting and debugging. In normal operation these records do not contain the text you write.

Recommendation: Do not put sensitive data such as ID numbers, passwords, or health or customer information in your prompts. The app warns you on tasks that may be sensitive and suggests using placeholders.

What we don’t do

  • There are no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or third-party trackers.
  • Personal data is not sold.
  • No account, e-mail address or sign-up is required to use the app.
  • Access to location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera is not requested.

Links to other tools

Pusula AI introduces tools such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini and links to those tools’ websites. When you open a tool, that tool’s own privacy policy applies. Pusula AI is an independent guide app; it is not affiliated with the owners of the tools and brands mentioned.

Children

Pusula AI is not directed at children and does not collect identity information from any user.
