Getting started

  • Write your task — in the Start tab, describe the work you want to do in your own words, or tap one of the example tasks. If you’re not sure, use “Open the demo” to look through a sample recommendation and learning path.
  • Answer the short questions — if needed, you’re asked 2–4 questions; if you’re in a hurry you can tap “Skip questions”.
  • See your recommendation — the main tool, the reasoning behind it, its alternatives and privacy notes. If you don’t like the tool, you can change it and rebuild your path.
  • Follow your learning path — each step has a short lesson and a practice applied to your own task: write your prompt and move forward with “Evaluate prompt”, “Improve prompt” and “Try it together”; save a prompt you like as a template.
  • Lab — write a task and pick at least two tools to compare them side by side.

Frequently asked questions

  • Do I need to create an account? No. The app doesn’t ask for an account, an e-mail address or sign-up.
  • Is it paid? Pusula AI is free; there are no in-app purchases and no ads.
  • Do the recommended tools run inside the app? No. Pusula teaches you which tool to use and how; you use the recommended tool on its own website or in its own app. Some tools may have their own paid plans.
  • Where is my data? Your tasks, learning paths, practice and templates are stored only on your device. For details, see the Privacy Policy.
  • How do I delete my data? You can delete all your tasks, paths, practice and templates with “Reset data” at the bottom of the Progress tab. Deleting the app also removes all data.

Troubleshooting

  • “Couldn’t reach the Pusula server” warning — recommendations, evaluation and comparison need an internet connection. Check your connection and try again. In the “Data and server” section at the bottom of the Progress tab you can see the connection status and, if the address has been changed, tap “Back to default”.
  • “We’re going a little too fast” warning — too many requests were sent in a short time; wait a few seconds and try again.
  • The response was cut off midway — when the connection drops, the response received up to that point is shown; you can tap “Try it together” again.
  • Still not solved? Send us an e-mail with your device model and iOS version, and we’ll help.
