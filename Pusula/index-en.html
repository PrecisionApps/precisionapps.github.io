Pusula AI

Say what you want to get done; learn which AI to use, and how, by doing it.

Pusula AI is a calm guide for people who are new to AI and for anyone stuck on “which tool should I choose?”. You describe the work you want to do in your own words; Pusula analyzes it, recommends the tool that fits you along with its reasoning, and builds a personal learning path that teaches you while you do that work.

What’s inside

  • Unbiased tool recommendations — for writing, research, documents, images, video, data, code and presentations, you see which of ten popular AI tools fits your work, together with the alternatives and the points you should watch out for.
  • Learn by doing — in a learning path of 3–7 steps you write a prompt for your own task, evaluate it, improve it, and try out what kind of response it produces.
  • Comparison lab — write a task, pick the tools; see how well they fit and a ten-criterion capability table side by side.
  • Tool catalog and templates — the strengths and weaknesses of the tools in one place; the prompts you like close at hand as templates.
  • Your progress — the paths you’ve completed, the tools you’ve learned and the skills you’ve gained.

No account needed. No ads. Your tasks, learning paths and templates are stored on your device.
